# Gustav Voigts Centre store imagery and directory copy

This directory prototype has 31 business profiles, all with visible text and banners. **A site-wide centre photograph is not evidence of a specific tenant's shop.** Current source imagery needs written reuse permission from the respective rights holders before full production release.

## Tenant-specific published imagery

- Torga Optical. Exact Gustav Voigts branch image published on the official Torga Optical website. Eyewear display and staff photo: https://torgaoptical.co.za/images/thumbs/1384057_20220524_085328%20cropped.jpeg . Store listing: https://torga.com.na/gustav-voigts-centre . The official page lists Shop 6A and a more recent phone number than the legacy centre directory. **Confirm and reconcile the numbers with centre management.**
- Avani Hotel, Clicks, Flower Love and Wellness Centre. Published images appear on https://gustavvoigtscentre.com.na/ in the original site's centre gallery. Confirm whether Flower Love is the business pictured in the legacy gallery (labelled Flowerlab) before using the image as verified proof.
- All other listings. The banner rotates between actual photos of the *centre*, never presented as specific tenant premises. The public UI explicitly says it is a centre photo and not that store. Replace each with approved storefront/brand photography from owners when received.

## Logo sourcing

- Logo.dev provides candidates for 12 validated brand domains. The revised logo surface is **transparent** and much larger, preserving a brand mark's natural aspect ratio.
- When Logo.dev fails or the returned image is incorrect, the **full business name**, set in strong display typography, replaces it without a bordered badge.
- Do not silently download the first result from Google/Facebook/Instagram. Social media image URLs often expire or contain unrelated brands; use media only after verifying ownership and reuse rights.
- A Wikimedia Commons file called `Checkers logo.svg` belongs to an American restaurant chain, not Checkers South Africa. Deliberately exclude it from this project: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Checkers_logo.svg
- Torga's branch photo and official site are verified, but image-use permission still requires approval.

## Content accuracy and follow-ups

- Store names, levels and public telephone numbers currently come from the centre's historical published directory at https://gustavvoigtscentre.com.na/page/store-directory/ and need a current occupancy audit.
- The introduction for each shop uses only its category or well-supported public brand identity. Unknown services, stock, prices, hours and promotions are not invented.
- Torga Optical's current store page lists a newer number than the centre's original directory; retain the directory number clearly labelled for now and add the official store page link.
- High priority photo requests: Checkers, Mr Price, Ackermans, Pepstores, Kalahari Pharmacy, Standard Bank, Furnmart and other independent businesses. Ask each tenant for a high-resolution transparent SVG/PNG wordmark, one 1600×1000 storefront/brand photo and a short approved introduction.
- Before launch verify permission, crop, mobile sizes, title/alt copy and the business's current details.
